How to Play

Learn the basics of Card Stuff Yes.

Getting Started

Card Stuff Yes is a multiplayer card game where you build a deck, draw cards, manage your resources, and battle your opponent.

  1. Create or log into your account.
  2. Collect cards from packs.
  3. Build your deck.
  4. Enter a game.
  5. Use your cards and resources to defeat your opponent.

Deck Building

Your deck contains 60 cards.

A base card can normally appear up to 4 times in a deck.

Use the Deck Builder to add cards you own and save your deck before entering a battle.

Resources

Card Stuff Yes uses several different resources. Cards and abilities can require different resources to use.

Magik Used for drawing cards.
Astral Magik Used to play cards.
Gilded Magik Used for effects.
Blood Magik Used for special damage effects. It can provide additional kill or bleed damage.
Dark Magik Used for discard-related effects.

Cards

Cards can have multiple abilities. Each ability can have its own resource cost.

Card effects, costs, and other gameplay rules are displayed by the game when available.

Winning

Use your deck, resources, and card abilities to defeat your opponent.

More detailed battle rules will be added here as the battle system is finalized.

More Coming Soon

Card Stuff Yes is still being developed. New cards, game modes, social features, and additional mechanics will be added over time.
