    PLAYTEST RULES

    How this prototype works

    • Start with 30 health and draw five cards.
    • Gain Astral Magik each turn, up to six.
    • Attack cards deal damage; guard cards add shield.
    • Heal restores health, and draw cards refill your hand.
    • Defeat the rival by reducing its health to zero.

    This is a local solo prototype, not online multiplayer. Match progress is kept in this page only.
